After installing

The installer removes the quarantine attribute from the installed app and plug-ins, signs them ad hoc on this Mac (so Apple Silicon runs them) and restarts the Audio Unit registry. Restart your music software so it rescans its plug-ins.

If macOS still blocks Ohpe

Double-click Ohpe Gatekeeper Fix.command on the Ohpe disk image. It asks for your administrator password and repeats these steps for every installed Ohpe copy; you can run it as often as you like.

Uninstalling

Double-click Uninstall Ohpe.command on the disk image. It removes the app, the plug-ins and the installer receipts and keeps your own presets in ~/Library/Application Support/groovex/Ohpe unless you agree to delete them.
