Welcome to Ohpe @VERSION@

Ohpe is a synthesizer, sampler and tape workstation by groovex – free software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later.

This installer can install, individually selectable under Customize:

  • Standalone app → /Applications/Ohpe.app
  • VST3 plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Ohpe.vst3
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Ohpe.component

All parts are universal binaries for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or newer).

Please quit your music software before you continue.
