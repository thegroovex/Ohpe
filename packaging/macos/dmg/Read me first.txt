OHPE FOR macOS - READ ME FIRST
==============================

Contents of this disk image
---------------------------
  Ohpe-Installer.pkg            installs Ohpe (choose the parts you want):
                                  - Standalone app  -> /Applications
                                  - VST3 plug-in    -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
                                  - Audio Unit      -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Ohpe Gatekeeper Fix.command   makes an installed Ohpe openable if macOS still blocks it
  Uninstall Ohpe.command        removes Ohpe again (keeps your own presets unless you say so)
  LICENSE.txt                   GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later

Requirements: macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel (universal binaries).


1. Opening the installer (it is not signed by Apple)
----------------------------------------------------
Ohpe is free software and this installer is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. On the first
double-click macOS therefore says it "cannot be opened" or comes "from an unidentified developer".
Use ONE of these ways - all are safe and only affect this one file:

  a) In Finder, right-click (or Control-click) "Ohpe-Installer.pkg" -> Open -> Open.

  b) Or open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about
     "Ohpe-Installer.pkg" and click "Open Anyway", then confirm.

  c) Or in Terminal (copy the pkg to your Downloads folder first):
         xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Ohpe-Installer.pkg
     then double-click the pkg as usual.

Then follow the installer. Under "Customize" you can switch the app, the VST3 and the Audio Unit
on or off individually.


2. What the installer does for Gatekeeper
-----------------------------------------
After copying, the installer automatically
  - removes the quarantine attribute from the installed app and plug-ins,
  - signs them "ad hoc" on your Mac, so Apple Silicon Macs run them,
  - restarts the Audio Unit registry, so your music software finds the AU.
Usually nothing else is needed. Restart your music software after installing.


3. If macOS still blocks Ohpe
-----------------------------
Double-click "Ohpe Gatekeeper Fix.command". It asks for your administrator password and repeats
the steps above for every installed Ohpe copy. You can run it as often as you like.
If macOS also blocks the .command file itself: right-click it -> Open -> Open, or run it in
Terminal:   bash "/Volumes/Ohpe/Ohpe Gatekeeper Fix.command"


4. Uninstalling
---------------
Double-click "Uninstall Ohpe.command" (same tip as above if macOS blocks it). It removes the app,
both plug-ins and the installer receipts. Your own presets in
~/Library/Application Support/groovex/Ohpe are kept unless you agree to delete them.
